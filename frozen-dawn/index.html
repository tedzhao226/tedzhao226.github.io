01

Crash Site → Ice Caves → Morgue

Open Thule & collect the first pieces

0:06–1:40

Open on YouTube · 0:06 ↗

Use the video’s early-round route: begin several weapon quests together and keep moving the battery forward.

Loadout Creator’s recommendation
Starting weapon 9mm SAP
Specialist Frontline
Mods Determination · Hand to Hand · Vicious
Equipment Sticky grenades
  1. On round 1, collect the wires and battery at Crash Site. Carry the battery to the Thule entrance. Save your starting points; the creator delays Quick Revive.
  2. Stand on the circular floor plate and melee the five zombies while on it. The entrance opens. Move down, buy the next door and kill the central special enemy.
  3. Stand in the first blood pool on the left and make five kills. In this route, four are available now; finish it with the first zombie of round 2.
  4. During the round change, move the battery to the Morgue door on the right. Pick up stone 1 by the Overlook ledge.
  5. Earn 1,250 points, open the Morgue, and get five kills in its blood pool. Take stone 2 to the right of its armor station.
  6. Move the battery toward the Phylactery door. Collect the Bloodraven stone on the left. This is a separate item from the four puzzle stones; save it for the third pool.
  7. On round 3, earn points and leave one zombie alive. Enter the Phylactery and move the battery toward the Overlook door. Buy Quick Revive when you can afford it before the lockdown.

Before moving onTwo pools filled; wires, Bloodraven stone and two puzzle stones collected. Battery moved toward Overlook.

02

Phylactery → Overlook → Archives → Blood Altar

Claim the scythe & finish the stone set

1:40–2:42

Open on YouTube · 1:40 ↗
  1. Interact with the forge symbol in the Phylactery to begin the scythe lockdown. Survive for about 30 seconds. Avoid unnecessary kills: this lockdown can advance rounds quickly. Pick up the scythe afterward.
  2. Open the Overlook and put the battery in its cauldron. Get about 15 nearby kills until the battery glows purple by itself.
  3. Open the Thulian Archives. Take stone 3 from the ground to the right of Speed Cola.
  4. Open the Blood Altar. Take stone 4 from the ledge. While here, check the possible gear on the ledge right of the Mystery Box and the book near the blood pool.

Before moving onBase scythe acquired; battery charged; all four puzzle stones collected.

03

Overlook · Rune order + four rotating pillars

Solve the stones & bring the hammer home

2:42–4:58

Open on YouTube · 2:42 ↗
  1. Insert the four stones into the Overlook wall slots. Read the symbols under them left to right. This is your game’s code.
  2. Find the matching symbols on the rocks around the middle. Kill one zombie beside each symbol in the wall’s order. Check that lightning enters the correct symbol after every kill. The creator’s M → F → S → R is only an example.
  3. When lightning flashes in the center, solve each of the four pillars using the table below. All four blocks on each pillar must face inward.
  4. Find the floating hammer around the main loop. Go prone before approaching it, crawl underneath and interact. Approaching upright sends it elsewhere.
  5. Guide it back to the Overlook center. It moves away from you, so stand on the opposite side from the direction you want it to travel. Pick it up when it reaches the center.

Pillar reference

Name the blocks A, B, C, D from top to bottom. First align the top three: shoot B until A faces inward, then C until B faces inward, then D until C faces inward. Now inspect the bottom block D.

D’s remaining direction Shoot A Shoot B Shoot C Shoot D
Right 1 2 3 0
Away from center 2 0 2 0
Left 3 2 1 0
Already inward 0 0 0 0

Use the direction shown from the pillar’s inward-facing side. Fire individual shots. A finished pillar glows purple; repeat for all four.

Before moving onFour pillars glow purple; base hammer acquired at Overlook.

04

Archives · Cipher Room · Gearworks · Orrery

Collect the books & gears; unlock the flail

4:58–6:55

Open on YouTube · 4:58 ↗
  1. Find two books among the four possible spots below. Insert them into the pedestal in the upper Archives to open the Cipher Room.
  2. Find three gears. Check the Cipher Room after opening it. The narration says “six” but then lists the seven candidates shown below.
  3. Install the three gears in the Gearworks. Kill zombies nearby, either downstairs by the gears or upstairs in the main Archives, until the Orrery activates.
  4. Read the colored orb positions on the cipher wall. Match each orb’s position on the Orrery using the orientation chart below.
  5. Interact with the matching colored control orb at the lower right as the moving orb approaches its target. There are eight positions: four cardinal directions and four diagonals. A wrong solution resets after a short delay.
  6. When all orbs match, take the Broken Flail.

05

Interleave three transport rides with the flail plates

Raise Pack-a-Punch & trace three constellations

6:55–9:15

Open on YouTube · 6:55 ↗
  1. Equip the scythe and go to the Passage. Interact with the wall block until it turns red, then take the transport into Lost in Blood. Preserve the normal zombies in that room.
  2. After being returned, two Corpse Eaters appear. Swing the scythe while holding Interact to take a spine. The creator describes one chance per enemy. Place the scythe at its Phylactery upgrade spot; the wires collected at spawn are part of this setup.
  3. Take the flail. Aim into the room above the Archives Orrery, press the grenade/equipment button to throw its orb, then press again to teleport. Stand on the left plate and get three flail melee kills. A rune floats above it.
  4. With the flail equipped, search the six viewpoints below while holding ADS / aim. When stars appear, keep aiming and move the crosshair over every star until the pattern lights up and completion is confirmed.
  5. Take the Blood Altar transport for the second Pack-a-Punch visit. Preserve the room’s normal zombies; kill the Corpse Eaters after returning. Go back above the Orrery, get three flail kills on the middle plate, then find and trace the next constellation.
  6. Take the Crash Site transport for the third visit. Again preserve normal zombies in Lost in Blood and kill the returning Corpse Eaters. Get three flail kills on the right plate, then trace the final constellation.

Six constellation viewpoints

These are the video’s search viewpoints, not six fixed answers. Only an activated constellation is visible through the flail’s aim view. Complete one plate and one constellation before starting the next. The creator does not give a reliable rune-to-location mapping.

Before moving onAll three constellations complete; Orrery glows; Pack-a-Punch raised through three transport visits.

06

Archives Orrery → Trial → Ice Caves

Pass the Moonraven / flail trial

9:15–9:51

Open on YouTube · 9:15 ↗
  1. At the glowing Orrery, throw the flail orb into the statue and teleport as the orb reaches it.
  2. Survive the trial’s fire walls by teleporting through them. As a wall approaches, aim the orb at the far side of the platform beyond the approaching wall. Throw just before the fire reaches the platform; teleport when the wall is near the platform’s middle.
  3. Complete this dodge twice, dealing with the zombies between attacks. The fire is lethal if the timing fails.
  4. Take the upgraded flail and place it in its Ice Caves pedestal.

Before moving onMoonraven trial complete; upgraded flail deposited in the Ice Caves.

07

Crash Site → Tunnel → Morgue → Phylactery → Blood Altar hall

Carry the lightning & solve the hammer wall

9:51–12:12

Open on YouTube · 9:51 ↗
  1. Take the hammer and buy Stamin-Up at Overlook. Melee the lightning rune at Crash Site to pick up its charge.
  2. Sprint to the next rune on the left side of the tunnel and melee it to deposit the charge. Melee again to pick it up and run to the rune on the Morgue wall beside the blood pool. Each deposited rune is a checkpoint; a timeout returns the charge to the last one.
  3. For the high Phylactery rune, first gather slower zombies on the left side of that room. Retrieve the charge, stand in the marked circle and melee a zombie beside it with clear line of sight to the high rune. A zombie underneath the door frame will not work. Lightning must jump to the rune.
  4. Creator’s shortcut: quickly put the hammer away and draw it again. If its lightning returns, melee the puzzle-wall rune in the hall toward Blood Altar.
  5. If the shortcut fails: place the Bloodraven stone in the Overlook’s third pool and get five kills. A shield-bearing Wüstling appears in the Blood Altar pool. Lead it beneath the Phylactery lightning, let it become charged, then kill it with the hammer. Pick up the charge from that kill and melee the puzzle-wall rune. It also drops the base shield.
  6. Solve the three wall patterns using the numbered charts below. Wait for every block to stop spinning before firing the next shot. If you make a mistake, shoot the skull block to reset.

08

Overlook → Stormraven trial → Blood pools

Pass the hammer trial & begin the shield upgrade

12:12–13:25

Open on YouTube · 12:12 ↗
  1. At Overlook, line up with the armor station and the center line of the volcano, as shown below. Hold the hammer out and walk straight forward. Do not steer left or right on the invisible path; continue until the trial transports you.
  2. In the trial, hold the grenade/equipment button to throw the hammer. This breaks the zombies’ shields. Finish the enemies and deposit the upgraded hammer in the Ice Caves.
  3. If still unfinished, put the Bloodraven stone into the Overlook pool and get five kills. Kill the shield-bearing Wüstling that emerges at Blood Altar and take its shield.
  4. Interact with the raven statue at the Overlook pool. Then inspect the three blood pools: the target has red sparks / sizzling energy. A still pool is not the current target.
  5. Listen for a raven, check the location atlas below, and shoot it with the 9mm SAP, following the creator’s method. This spawns a charged Corpse Eater. Keep it close and bring it to the active pool.

Before moving onUpgraded hammer deposited; shield acquired; raven statue activated; current energized pool identified.

10

Three charged Corpse Eaters → Radio symbols → Bloodraven trial

Record the pool order & complete the shield

15:02–17:17

Open on YouTube · 15:02 ↗
  1. Keep the charged Corpse Eater close enough that it does not despawn. Lead it to the currently sizzling pool, stand in the pool, and melee it with the shield equipped. The creator’s example takes two hits. Move away from its death explosion to avoid losing armor.
  2. A symbol appears in the pool. Record which pool was first; its symbol can be revisited. Find the next energized pool, shoot another raven, and repeat. Do this three times, recording the pool order.
  3. At Crash Site, lead a bomber zombie to the metal panel near Quick Revive. Stand on the opposite side so its explosion breaks the metal. Pick up both the radio and the separate speaker.
  4. Install the radio and speaker on their two rocks at the Blood Altar. Interacting with the radio cycles the symbol in the large pool.
  5. Revisit your first charged-enemy pool and inspect its symbol. Select that exact symbol with the radio, then kill one zombie in the large Blood Altar pool to submit it. Repeat for your second and third pools, in that order.
  6. After a correct sequence, the large pool flashes through multiple symbols. Only after this confirmation, let yourself be downed while standing in it to enter the shield trial. The creator says this scripted transition does not consume Quick Revive.
  7. Get shield kills in the trial. When the area attack comes, stand on the plate and hold ADS / aim to block. Do this twice. Deposit the upgraded shield in the round floor slot at the Ice Caves weapon ledge.

The radio solution depends on your pool order and your symbols. Similar-looking symbols are easy to confuse. Compare the actual line shapes; the source screenshots are examples, not a universal three-symbol password.

11

Phylactery forge → Two round changes → Deathraven trial

Finish the scythe & prepare for the boss

17:17–18:10

Open on YouTube · 17:17 ↗
  1. Return to the scythe left at its Phylactery upgrade spot. Kill zombies nearby until it glows. Then let two rounds pass.
  2. During that wait, prepare: fully overcharged red Frontline meter, full armor, and a Pack-a-Punched 9mm SAP. The creator suggests the melee perk, Stamin-Up, Quick Revive and Double Tap. Jack-in-the-Boxes are optional.
  3. After two rounds, pick up the scythe. It is temporarily unusable. Take a Pack-a-Punch transport to enter its trial.
  4. Kill the enemies with the scythe. The creator switches to a firearm for remaining Wüstlings when easier. Complete the trial and place the upgraded scythe in the last Ice Caves weapon slot.
  5. Check that all four weapons have been deposited: flail, hammer, shield and scythe. All four fires around the volcano ignite and dialogue plays.

Before moving onFour weapons deposited; four fires lit; boss transport opened.

12

Final check → Scythe + Frontline → Kingfall

Enter the God-King fight

18:10–18:49

Open on YouTube · 18:10 ↗

Finish your preparation before entering the new transport. This section follows the video’s fast solo scythe / Frontline method; its success depends on reaching the fight with that setup.

  1. After the four fires light and the dialogue plays, pick the upgraded scythe back up. Confirm full armor and an overcharged red Frontline meter.
  2. Enter the newly opened boss transport shown below. A cutscene begins the encounter.
  3. Run to the God-King, activate Frontline and repeatedly melee him with the upgraded scythe. Follow him when he moves.
  4. If he is over a plate and cannot be hit, quickly kill nearby zombies, then resume attacking when he returns to his chair.
  5. Continue through the next cutscene. In the creator’s run, Frontline remains active afterward; keep attacking with the scythe until the final defeat and Kingfall achievement.

Before moving onGod-King defeated; Kingfall unlocked.
